# Clinical and content boundaries

Nayacura is a design and engineering demo. It is not a clinic, not a medical provider and not a source of personalised medical advice.

## What the demo represents

- Sexual wellness, relationship counselling and emotional wellbeing as connected areas of care.
- A consent-led, nonjudgmental tone.
- Clear pathways to qualified professionals.
- Cautious education that acknowledges uncertainty.
- Privacy-minded data practices and explicit boundaries.

## What the demo does not represent

- Real clinicians, real credentials, real registration numbers or real clinic locations.
- Real patient stories, reviews or outcomes.
- Real medical review. Articles are marked as unreviewed drafts.
- Real diagnosis, prescription, treatment or emergency response.
- Any accreditation, certification, partnership or regulatory approval.

## Fictional clinician data

The eight public clinician profiles and one pending profile are fictional. Their names, illustrated portraits, qualifications, registration numbers, fees, availability, clinic names and cities are demo data.

Portraits are original SVG illustrations generated by this project. They are not photographs of real people. This is a deliberate choice: using a real person’s face for a fictional clinician would imply an identity and credential that do not exist.

Production requirements:

1. Primary-source identity and registration verification with the relevant council.
2. Documented credential expiry and re-verification.
3. Verified clinic addresses and contact details, shared only through appropriate channels.
4. Transparent fees and cancellation terms.
5. Professional indemnity and employment/contractor arrangements reviewed by counsel.
6. Clinician training on consent, telemedicine limits, safeguarding and emergency escalation.

## Patient stories and reviews

The homepage uses the label **“Illustrative composite · not a real testimonial”** for every story. No reviews are seeded. A review can be submitted only after a completed appointment and is stored as `PENDING` for moderation. Ratings are calculated only from approved reviews.

Do not add invented testimonials, before/after claims, fake star ratings or outcome statistics.

## Article policy

Articles are original educational drafts. They:

- Avoid diagnosis and personalised treatment advice.
- Name uncertainty where evidence is nuanced.
- Encourage professional assessment for personal concerns.
- Include emergency guidance where relevant.
- Do not invent reviewers or medical credentials.
- Are displayed with an explicit “Editorial draft / not medically reviewed” label.

Production workflow should include:

- A named, credential-verified medical reviewer.
- Documented review date and version.
- Clinical sign-off before publication.
- Scheduled re-review.
- Corrections log.
- Clear separation between general education and advertising.
- Native-language review, including Tamil.

## AI boundaries

Naya AI:

- Clearly identifies itself as AI, not a doctor.
- Is instructed not to diagnose, prescribe, guarantee outcomes or provide emergency care.
- Intercepts emergency terms in English and Tamil and returns fixed escalation guidance.
- Recommends qualified professional support for personal medical concerns.
- Does not claim access to clinical records.
- Stores conversations only with explicit consent and supports deletion.

Naya is not a clinically validated triage or crisis system. The safety keyword layer is a demo safeguard, not a comprehensive classifier. A production system requires clinical safety review, adversarial testing, red-teaming, monitoring and a human escalation pathway.

## Consent model

- **Privacy notice / terms:** recorded at registration.
- **Teleconsultation:** recorded before a video room token is issued; states the limits of remote care.
- **Recording:** not offered in this demo; no recording by default.
- **AI storage:** opt-in per conversation; refusal does not prevent chatting.
- **Marketing:** separate opt-in, not bundled with care.
- **Documents:** patient explicitly uploads; access limited to care team.

## Medical emergency guidance

The platform is not an emergency service. The UI states:

- Call local emergency services (112 in India).
- Go to the nearest emergency department for severe symptoms.
- Tele-MANAS **14416** for mental health crisis support in India.
- Do not wait for an online appointment or AI response.

## Content tone rules

- No shame-based messaging.
- No explicit sexual imagery or erotic content.
- No promises of cure or guaranteed outcomes.
- No fake statistics.
- No moral judgment about relationship structure, identity or sexual history.
- No assumption that a partner, marriage or particular family form is the goal.
- Use “may”, “can”, “some people” and “a clinician can assess” rather than certainty.
- Distinguish general education from individual clinical advice.

## Legal drafts

All nine legal pages are marked **pre-launch draft** and require review by qualified Indian legal and clinical professionals. Do not publish them as binding policies without review. In particular, the final documents must name:

- The operating legal entity and registered address
- The grievance officer and contact channel
- Actual data processors and cross-border transfer safeguards
- Retention periods and lawful bases
- The clinical complaints process
- Payment, refund and tax terms
- The applicable regulatory and dispute-resolution framework

No claim of HIPAA, GDPR, DPDP, ABDM or any other certification/compliance should be made unless independently verified.
